Hengjie Liu

Postdoctoral Scholar · UCSF Radiation Oncology

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in Radiation Oncology at the University of California, San Francisco. I received my Ph.D. in Physics & Biology in Medicine from UCLA and my B.S. in Engineering Physics from Tsinghua University. My research broadly focuses on computational methods for medical imaging.

My research training has spanned two fairly different areas. I spent about four years working on MRI physics, acquisition, and reconstruction, including 4D MRI, 4D flow MRI, pulse-sequence development, and low-field MRI systems. I subsequently spent about four years working on deformable image registration, with an emphasis on learning- and optimization-based methods, registration-specific design, multimodal registration, and anatomically plausible deformation.

Moving between these areas required me to learn new tools and ways of thinking. I am particularly interested in problems at the intersection of medical imaging, machine learning, numerical optimization, and imaging physics, and in understanding when a computational method works—not only whether it works.
